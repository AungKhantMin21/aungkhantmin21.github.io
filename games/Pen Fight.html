Pen Fight

Take turns flicking your pen. Knock the others off the desk. If yours falls, you're out. Last pen standing wins.

  • Without warning, an edge of the desk can crack off.
  • Slide over an ink blot to grab a power: Double flick, Heavy hit or Shield.
  • Smash the eraser, sharpener and coin around. Each has its own weight and bounce.
  • "Teacher's coming!" You get 3 seconds to flick or lose your turn.
Players

To flick, put your finger anywhere, drag back like a slingshot, and let go. Touch near the end of your pen to make it spin.
